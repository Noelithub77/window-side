01 Why

Dual boot is a wall.

Switching means a reboot. A fresh VM means a second Windows to set up. window-side opens the one you have.

  2. Second copy

03 GPU passthrough

Your whole GPU,
in a window.

Windows owns the whole graphics card, not a slice of it, so games run at full frame rate. Looking Glass shows them in a Linux window, and a normal boot gives the card back.

Looking Glass is by Geoffrey McRae and contributors.

04 Sign-in

Signed in
before you look.

Save your password once in your Linux keyring. window-side types it in when Windows starts.
